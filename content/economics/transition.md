title: 'Transition'

By 2050, 90% of jobs will be able to done by AI.

Gradual de-evaluation of resources is the way if we want to eliminate humans from the workforce. We can't de-evalue everything at once, even if we hypothetically had the power to do so, because doing so will suddenly erode value for resource owners & hence will lead to resistance & unrest from them.

To keep the society functional, we need to ensure that by the time there are no jobs, basic needs of any human are fulfilled-
1. Food
2. Clothing
3. Housing
4. Mobility

In terms of effort, clothing < mobility < food << housing, because of the amount of wealth invested in this sector. We will need to find an alternative wealth holder to compensate people for their resources- something which is finite, could be bitcoin.

I think of this process as a directed graph, ie, to make production cheap, we will first need to invest in chip manufacturing & factory producing robots & energy generators like solar & wind.

If planned properly, this could be our species chance at eternal peace & prosperity. I left unchecked, it could very quickly become a dystopia where the rich & the powerful hog resources & create armies to protect themselves.
