title: 'Revival of the Dream'
---

Internet was never meant to be a dozen mega-corporations owning a majority of space. It was supposed to be quite decentralized. With the advent of AI, code has become cheap & soon it will be ubiquitous. Internet will soon be what it was always supposed to be- a space for everyone, & a fiefdom of none.
