The only 2 roles I foresee remaining is that of a builder & a maintainer, & deployment is the boundary which separates both.

The only good is that the loop will close on us (senior/staff engineers)- junior hiring is already down & roles high up the ladder like principal, VP etc

How will each sector fare-
1. Rise of boutique consultancies all over the world
2. related to previous point, Offshoring for cost arbitrage will die- code is already cheap, taste is what matters more
3. Decline of SaaS as enterprises implement their own custom solutions, which might be derived some general open sourced solutions
4. Cloud/SRE might not be affected.
