**Live:** [p2p-mock.vercel.app](https://p2p-mock.vercel.app)

Mock interviews are most useful when they come from people who do the job, and hardest to arrange when you have to ask strangers. P2P Mock Interview is built around the people you're already one step away from: your **2nd-degree connections**.

## The idea

- Practice interviewing with peers instead of paying for coaching or cold-messaging strangers.
- A shared connection makes it easier to ask, and easier to say yes.
- Everyone takes turns being the candidate and the interviewer, so both sides improve.

## Under the hood

- **Frontend:** Next.js / TypeScript, deployed on Vercel.
- **Backend:** deployed separately on Railway.

The repo is private.
